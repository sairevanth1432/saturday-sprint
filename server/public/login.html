Saturday
Sprint

Learn a few focused topics during the week, practise them, then show what you learnt in a timed End-of-Sprint Test on Saturday. The test is built on the learning units in this portal.

What to do as a student

Five steps from registering to your rank on the leaderboard.

  1. 1Register once with your NIAT ID, your own mobile number and a password, then enter the code sent to your phone.
  2. 2Wait for a mentor to approve you. After that, log in with your NIAT ID and password.
  3. 3Finish the learning units in Learn. Each unit is Watch → Play → Read.
  4. 4Practise every topic in Practice: quizzes and coding problems, with explanations.
  5. 5Take the End-of-Sprint Test on Saturday. It is timed and proctored, and you get one attempt. Then see your rank.

What to do as a mentor

Mentors approve students, follow their progress and run the Sprint from the mentor console.

  1. 1Get your account from the program super admin, then log in at the mentor console with your email, password and an authenticator app.
  2. 2Approve registrations in Approvals. Check that the NIAT ID and name belong to your student.
  3. 3Follow the learning in Students (each student's progress) and Practice analytics (which questions the batch gets wrong). Reteach those topics.
  4. 4Help students get in: change a login phone number or reset a password from the student's page.
  5. 5On Sprint day, follow Results live: proctoring flags and each attempt's activity timeline. Then share the Leaderboard.
