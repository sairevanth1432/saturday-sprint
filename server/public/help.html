How the Sprint works

Everything you need to know as a student: what to do in the portal, what each part is for, and how the End-of-Sprint Test on Saturday works. It takes about five minutes to read.

01 · overview

What is Saturday Sprint?

Saturday Sprint is a short, focused learning sprint. During the week you work through a few learning units in this portal: you watch a short video, play an interactive game, and read the notes. You practise each topic with quizzes and coding problems. Then, on Saturday, you take a timed End-of-Sprint Test to show what you learnt, and see where you stand on the leaderboard.

There are two courses: Programming Foundations (Python) and Intro to GenAI.

02 · purpose

Why we run a Sprint

A small, clear goal

A handful of topics in one week is easy to finish. You always know what to do next and when you are done.

Learning by doing

Each topic is seen three ways (watch, play, read) and then practised. That is how it sticks, instead of just reading slides.

Know what you really know

The test shows which topics you have mastered and which need another look, while there is still time to fix them.

Friendly competition

The leaderboard turns the week into a challenge. Score first, then speed: careful work beats guessing.

Better help from mentors

Mentors see which practice questions the batch finds hardest and use that to plan the next session.

Practice for real assessments

The test runs like a hiring or competitive assessment: timed, in full screen, one attempt. A safe place to get used to that.

03 · content

The Sprint is built on the learning units

The End-of-Sprint Test covers the topics of the learning units in the portal, and nothing else. Every unit has three steps, in this order:

1 · Watch2 · Play3 · Read
  • Watch: a short video that explains the idea.
  • Play: an interactive game where you try it yourself, level by level.
  • Read: notes to revise from. Some units show "coming soon" until the notes are added.

Programming Foundations

  1. Nested Conditional Statements
  2. For Loop
  3. Comparing Strings & Naming Variables

Intro to GenAI

  1. Generative AI Foundations
  2. Social Media Content Automation Workflow
  3. AI News Summarizer
The simple rule: finish every unit (all three steps) and the practice questions for its topic before Saturday. Then you have seen everything the test asks about.

04 · what to do

Your path, step by step

  1. Log in with your NIAT ID and nameOn the log-in page, type your NIAT ID and your name as it is in the college list. Capital letters and word order do not matter. No registration, phone or password is needed. Your progress is saved to your account, so you can continue on any computer.
  2. Register onceOn the Register tab, enter your NIAT ID, your own mobile number and a password. Type the 6-digit code sent to your phone.
  3. Wait for approvalA mentor checks your NIAT ID against the student list and approves you, usually within a day. Until then, logging in shows "waiting for approval".
  4. Log in with your NIAT ID and passwordNo code is needed after that. Your progress is saved to your account, so you can continue on any computer.
  5. Complete the learning unitsLearn → pick a course → open each unit and go Watch → Play → Read. A ✓ marks every finished step.
  6. Practise every topicPractice → choose the course and topic → answer the quizzes and solve the coding problems. Each answer shows the explanation straight away.
  7. Take the End-of-Sprint TestOn Saturday, during the test window: Sprint test → read the rules → Start. You get one attempt.
  8. See your score and rankYour score appears as soon as you submit. Leaderboard shows your rank in the batch.

05 · the portal

What you can do in the portal

~/home

Home

Where you are this week: your level and XP, what to do next, the Sprint countdown and the top of the leaderboard.

~/learn

Learn

The two courses and their units. Use Next and ← Previous to move between steps and units. Videos have play, pause and seek controls.

~/practice

Practice

Quizzes for every topic, with an explanation after each answer. Your first answer counts towards your practice score, so think before you click. Skip any question and come back later.

~/code

Write code

Coding problems for the Programming topics. Write Python in the browser, press Run to try it with your own input, then Submit to check it against the test cases.

~/sprint-test

Sprint test

The End-of-Sprint Test. It opens only during the test window; before that you see when it opens and how long it lasts.

~/leaderboard

Leaderboard

Ranks after the test, by score, then by time taken. Only part of each NIAT ID is shown. It refreshes on its own.

Levels and XP: every finished lesson gives 20 XP and every correct practice answer 10 XP. You move up from Rookie to Coder (120 XP), Hacker (300 XP) and Architect (500 XP).

  • Each question is worth 1 mark. A wrong answer does not cost marks, so answer every question.
  • The timer starts when you press Start and cannot be paused. If you start late, you still have to finish before the window closes.
  • Your answers save automatically. If the page reloads or your internet drops, open the portal again: your answers and the timer are still there.
  • Move between questions with the numbers on the right, and change any answer until you submit.
  • When the time is up, the test is submitted for you with the answers you have.
  • Ranking: higher score first. Equal scores are ranked by time taken (less is better).

07 · proctoring

Test rules

The test is proctored, like a hiring assessment. Before you start, the portal shows these rules again.

  • The test runs in full screen on a computer. Leaving full screen counts as a violation.
  • Leaving the test tab, switching to another app or window, or opening the test in a second tab or device counts as a violation.
  • Each violation shows a warning with your count. After 3 violations the test is submitted automatically with the answers you have.
  • Copying, selecting text, right-click and printing are turned off during the test.
  • Your NIAT ID is shown over the questions, and mentors can see a record of your test activity.
  • While the test is running you cannot open Learn, Practice or Help. Finish and submit first.
Before you press Start: use a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge, charge it or plug it in, close other apps and chat windows, turn off notifications, and check your internet. Keep your phone away.

08 · help

Questions & help

My NIAT ID and name are not accepted

Type your name exactly as it is in the college list, with all its words (for example "Asha Kumar", not just "Asha"). Check the NIAT ID for the digit 0 and the letter O. If it is still not accepted, your name may be spelt differently in the list: ask your mentor to check it.

I forgot my password

On the log-in page, choose Forgot password?. We send a code to your registered mobile number; enter it and choose a new password. Your other devices are signed out.

I registered before passwords existed / I have never set a password

Choose First time? Log in with a code, enter the code sent to your phone, and set your password. From then on you log in with your NIAT ID and password.

It says "waiting for approval"

A mentor has not approved your registration yet. Approvals are usually done within a day. If it takes longer, ask your mentor.

My NIAT ID is not accepted

Check it for typos (for example, the digit 0 and the letter O). If it is still not accepted, you may not be in the student list yet. Ask your mentor to add you.

I changed my phone number

Codes always go to the number you registered with. Ask your mentor to change your login phone number.

A video does not play

Press the play button on the video itself. If it still does not play, reload the page, update your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari), or try another network. Tell your mentor which unit it was.

My internet dropped during the test

Your answers up to the last save are kept, and the timer keeps running on the server. Reconnect and open the portal again as soon as you can. You will see your answers and the time left.

I got a warning by mistake

Press the button on the warning to go back to the test, and stay in full screen. Mentors can see the full activity record if you need to explain what happened.

Can I take the test again?

No, there is one attempt per Sprint. Only the program admin can reset an attempt (for example after a technical problem), and only while the test window is open.

Where do I see my score?

Straight after you submit, on the test screen, and on the Leaderboard (when the mentors have made it visible).

Still stuck? Contact your mentor or program admin.

09 · mentors

For mentors

Mentors use the mentor console (admin console). Your account is created by the program's super admin; you log in with your email, your password and an authenticator app.

  • Approvals: approve student registrations after checking the NIAT ID and name.
  • Master data: keep the student list right. Students log in with the NIAT ID and the name written there.
  • Student analytics (super admin): time spent, units opened, videos watched and logins for every student.
  • Students: each student's progress, account, phone number and password reset.
  • Practice analytics: how many students attempted each practice question and how many got it right. Use it to find the topics to reteach.
  • Results: the test attempts with their proctoring flags and activity timeline. Leaderboard: the rankings, with a CSV export.
